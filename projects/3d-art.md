## Binary Series

*December, 2019*

Created a series of abstract clay sculptures out of 1s and 0s to depict emotions; illustrating how we get more from technology than just binary numbers. The binary numbers make us feel real emotions. Morphed 1s and 0s into forms that represent sadness, conflict, confusion and joy.
