Website Redesign for Santa Clara University's Outdoor Exploration Club (Adobe Illustrator)

Individual Project

## Project Overview

The goal of this project was to redesign a website for the SCU club Into the Wild that is more unique and follows a less standard template. The website should draw users in with its organic and minimalist design. It should stand out to the students so they remember the site and refer more people to it. The website should be a clear and easy way to get information about the club. Users should immediately feel the outdoor elements as they enter the site; instantly getting a feel for the club.

## Purpose of Website

The purpose of the site is to provide students with information about the club and motivate them to join the trips and meet new people. The site contains lots of useful content such as information on how to pack, current trips, and gear rentals. It also gives students an idea of what the community is like by showing the mission statement and values, the team, and their "Leaders in Training" program.

### Target Audience

- College Student (male or female)
- Between 18-25 years old
- Online frequency: high
- Online activities: Instagram, Twitter, Tik Tok, Facebook, Snapchat, Pinterest
- Interested in nature, hiking, traveling, meeting new people
- Cares about making friends, branching out, having fun, and making the most of their college experience

### Website Goals

- Easy to understand and navigate
- Quickly spark the user's interests
- Attract more viewers, leading to more club members
- Give students all of the necessary information and steps to become active participants
- Make students feel included, safe, and supported enough to explore

## My Design Choices

- My main goal for this website was to incorporate different elements of nature. When a student comes to the site they should immediately get a feel for the club.
- I wanted the site to be unique and stand out so that students remember the website and most importantly, remember the club.
- The current website follows a pretty standard template so I wanted to make it more creative by incorporating some modern aspects and organic shapes.
- I used lots of greens and blues to give it a natural feel. The blues also added an element of tranquility and peacefulness, while the greens gave it a happy, fresh, and earthy feeling.
- I made sure the website was consistent by having images at the top of each page. Initially, the Reading page did not have an image, but I added one to tie it all together.
- I wanted to better optimize the space by getting rid of the empty space on the sides of the initial site while still making sure it did not feel overly cluttered.
- I chose simpler images with less contrasting colors for the Reading page and created new flyer designs for the Trips page so that it felt less cluttered.
