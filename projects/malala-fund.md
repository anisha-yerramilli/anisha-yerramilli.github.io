Website Redesign for the Malala Fund Site (Adobe Illustrator)

Individual Project

## Project Overview

Over 130 million girls are not in school and Malala has made it her mission to give these girls the education they deserve. The Malala Fund invests in advocates and educators who can help these girls receive a secondary education. They also focus on advocating at all levels to make real changes in the system and acquire the necessary resources. The Malala Fund gives these girls a voice, sharing their stories in their digital publication and newsletter.

## Purpose of Website

The site's purpose is to educate users about the challenges these girls are facing and encourage them to help out with a donation. The website also provides users with many other volunteer opportunities. Malala realizes the importance of sharing her story because many girls are in similar situations and this reality needs to be known. She says, "I tell my story not because it is unique, but because it is the story of many girls."

### Target Audience

- Occupation: stay-at-home mom
- Age: in her 40s and 50s
- Gender: female
- Online frequency: medium
- Online activities: Facebook, Amazon shopping, recipes, emails, researching volunteer opportunities, reading the news
- Someone who is not working and wants to give back to the community in some way.

### Website Goals

- Educate users about the struggles these girls are facing
- Have users develop some kind of connection to these stories
- Get users motivated to help in whatever way they can

## My Design Choices

- My main goal for this website was to make it stand out. For how important the cause is, I wanted the website to be more impactful.
- The original site felt too cluttered so I removed photographs and text that did not seem necessary.
- I made the site more concise and tried to best optimize the space. In certain areas, there was too much white space and the user had to scroll down too much in order to get all of the information.
- I eliminated harsh lines and used organic shapes to make it flow easily - originally the main image on the homepage seemed disconnected from the rest of the page.
- I used bright, vibrant colors to make the site stand out. One of the main colors I used was red, which represents strength and courage. Orange is another main color, which represents energy.
