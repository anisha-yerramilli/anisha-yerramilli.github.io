App Design to Create a Virtual Campus Experience (Figma)

Group Project - 4 Members

Role: User interviews, usability tests, persona creation, mockups

## Problem Statement

Many Santa Clara University undergraduate students and faculty have found that communication and collaboration have been harder without the ease of in-person interaction as a result of the COVID-19 pandemic and shelter-in-place order. There needs to be a way to provide a comprehensive social platform that replicates in-person interaction to allow for simple, easy, and efficient communication and collaboration. It should be accessible to SCU faculty and students who are unable to be on campus/in-person for a variety of reasons (aimed for current pandemic but could have other uses afterward).

## Solution Statement

An app that focuses on attending live synchronous lectures, office hours, and study groups. There will also be built-in direct messaging to help facilitate communications and it is designed to be more instantaneous than email. This app is not to organize when you have class and office hours, but rather as a means to consolidate all meetings into one platform.

## Usability Testing Results

Initially, we used red to indicate the room's inactiveness, but it was perceived as a caution or warning towards the room. The use of red was also confusing for the Direct Messaging page where we tried to show hierarchy between groups, like professors and TAs versus students. To fix the issue with color, we **changed all the inactive rooms to be a single color: gray** and kept the active rooms as green. To show categories or hierarchy on the Direct Messaging page, we changed it so there are **categories rather than color-coded messages**.

Similarly, we only relied on color cues and each room still said "Join Room" which told the user they could join an inactive room, even when they could not. As a result, we **changed the button text of inactive rooms to "Inactive"** so it explicitly shows the room is not active. For study groups, we added a message that says "Be the first to join" to indicate the user can still join.

The homepage was also unclear since it only said "upcoming" but did not display any dates or times for when classes, office hours, or messages were happening or delivered. This made it difficult for users to know when events were happening so **we added dates and times to clarify**.
